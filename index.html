Bible Verses

Bible Verses is a personal tool that uploads Bible verse videos to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

What the app does

  • Signs in with Google to get permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • Uploads videos, along with their titles and descriptions, to that channel.

The app does not read, change, or delete any other data in your Google account.
