Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy describes how the Bible Verses app ("the app") handles information when you sign in with your Google account.

Information the app accesses

The app requests the YouTube upload permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload). This is used only to upload videos, and their titles and descriptions, to the YouTube channel you authorize.

How information is used

The app uses this access only to upload videos you have chosen to publish. It does not read, change, or delete your other YouTube content or any other Google account data.

Storage

The app stores the OAuth access token needed to upload on your behalf. It is kept on the device running the app and is never sold, shared, or sent to anyone other than Google.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Data is transferred only to Google/YouTube to complete uploads.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
